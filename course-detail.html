Call for Papers

Submission Guidelines

Format

  • 4 to 6 pages in Journal format.
  • Maximum 5–6 participants per paper.

Originality

By submitting, authors confirm that the manuscript is original, unpublished work that is not under evaluation elsewhere. Submissions must present original work with a clear problem description, an overview and comparison to related work, the theoretical framework or model proposed, experimental results, and conclusions.

Review process

All submissions will be reviewed by a committee to ensure that only high-quality papers with originality and novelty are accepted for presentation. Plagiarism in any form will lead to outright rejection. Non-compliance with these guidelines may result in rejection.

Who can submit

Students from Master's and Engineering programmes (MCA, M.Sc., M.E., M.Tech., M.Phil., B.E., B.Tech), PhD scholars, industry delegates, researchers, and scientists are all invited to submit.

Publication

All selected papers will be considered for publication as Scopus-indexed book chapters or in Scopus-indexed Q3/Q4 journals, subject to the quality and novelty of the research work. The final publication and indexing status will depend on the novelty, originality, quality, and scope of the submitted paper. Presented and selected papers have previously been published in Scopus, Springer, and Wiley.

After registration

After successful registration, you will receive a confirmation mail along with the link for paper submission.
