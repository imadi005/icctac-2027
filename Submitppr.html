Call for Papers

Paper Submission

How submission works

We invite students from Master's and Engineering programmes (MCA, M.Sc., M.E., M.Tech., M.Phil., B.E., B.Tech), PhD scholars, industry delegates, researchers, and scientists to submit original, unpublished contributions for presentation. Submissions must not be previously published or under evaluation elsewhere.

01
Prepare your manuscript in 4 to 6 pages, Journal format, following the guidelines on the Submission Guidelines page. Maximum 5–6 participants per paper.
02
Register via the official event portal and pay the applicable registration fee, listed on the registration section of the website.
03
Receive your confirmation email — after successful registration, you will receive a confirmation mail along with the link for paper submission.
04
Submit before the deadline — papers must be submitted on or before the full paper submission date listed under Important Dates.

What your paper should include

Papers should present original work with a clear problem description, an overview and comparison to related work, the theoretical framework or model proposed, experimental results, and conclusions. All submissions are reviewed by a committee to ensure only high-quality papers with originality and novelty are accepted. Plagiarism in any form will lead to outright rejection.

Publication

All selected papers will be considered for publication as Scopus-indexed book chapters or in Scopus-indexed Q3/Q4 journals, subject to the quality and novelty of the research work. Presented and selected papers have previously been published in Scopus, Springer, and Wiley.
